Case Study: Nexora, a Digital Savings and Financial Management Platform

Nature of the System

Nexora is a simulated digital savings and financial management platform where customers create savings accounts, set personal financial goals, deposit and withdraw money, monitor their savings progress, and view their transaction history. The platform is designed to help individuals manage their money, develop disciplined savings habits, and track their financial progress through a mobile or web application.

Users of the Database

* Customers (individuals and small business owners): Use the Nexora mobile/web application to manage savings accounts, create savings goals, deposit and withdraw funds, and view transaction records.
* Customer support staff: Handle customer enquiries, account access issues, transaction complaints, and savings-related disputes.
* Finance and operations officers: Monitor deposits, withdrawals, account balances, and interest calculations.
* Compliance and audit officers: Review customer verification records, audit transactions, monitor suspicious activities, and generate compliance reports.
* System administrators: Manage user permissions, system configurations, database access, and platform operations.
* System services: Process deposits and withdrawals, update account balances, calculate interest, send notifications, and generate periodic account statements.

Major Data Stored

* Customer records: Customer IDs, names, contact details, addresses, registration dates, and account status.
* Identity verification records: Verification status, identity document references, verification dates, and review outcomes.
* Savings accounts: Account numbers, account types, opening dates, available balances, and account status.
* Savings goals: Goal names, target amounts, current progress, target dates, and goal status.
* Transactions: Deposits, withdrawals, transfers, transaction references, amounts, dates, and transaction statuses.
* Savings plans: Plan types, minimum deposit requirements, interest rates, durations, and withdrawal conditions.
* Interest records: Interest rates applied, calculation periods, accrued amounts, and posting dates.
* Notifications: Transaction alerts, savings reminders, goal progress updates, and account notifications.
* Audit logs and risk records: Records of important account changes, administrative activities, failed transactions, and suspicious activity flags.

Key Operations Supported

* Register a customer and verify their identity.
* Create, update, and deactivate savings accounts.
* Create savings goals and track progress towards target amounts.
* Deposit money and update account balances.
* Process withdrawals while enforcing applicable account rules.
* Calculate and credit interest according to the relevant savings plan.
* Generate customer account statements and transaction histories.
* Send notifications for successful transactions, savings milestones, and account activities.
* Monitor failed or suspicious transactions and flag accounts for review.
* Generate reports on total savings, customer activity, interest accrued, and account balances.
* Maintain audit trails for accountability, security, and database integrity.
